# Map 2 phase simulation

Generated by `node scripts/simulate-map2.ts`. Bots see the game only through `observe()`. Stages are written M1 to M10 for the Moonlit list and C1 to C10 for the classic list.

## Phase difficulty

Mean rounds a player needs to complete a phase: rounds attempted divided by completions, 5000 rounds per phase. Lower is easier.

Each round has four Professor Hoots. One attempts the phase being measured; the other three play a fixed mix of the classic phases, the same for every phase measured. So every phase faces the same opponents, who end rounds at the same pace.

Two other setups were tried and set aside. With all four players on the phase being measured, how long a round lasts dominates: a phase that is hard to hit onto keeps the round going until nearly everyone lays down, so the hardest phase measured as the easiest. Measured inside whole matches, a phase comes out harder near the start of the list, where opponents lay down quickly, so its result depends on where it sits.

### Final order

Sorted easiest first. Then any two neighbours within 5% of each other go back into draft order, and the hardest phase always goes last. "Within 5%" is not transitive, so a chain like A within 5% of B, B within 5% of C, but A and C further apart can make the rules disagree. Where they do, the measured difficulty wins.
